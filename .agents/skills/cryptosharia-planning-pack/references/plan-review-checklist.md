# Plan Review Checklist

Use this checklist before asking for plan approval.

- Objective is one clear outcome with business or technical value.
- Scope and out-of-scope are explicit and testable.
- Acceptance criteria in `spec.md` are measurable.
- `execution.md` has allowed files and escalation rules.
- Verification commands are listed in `plan.md`.
- `tasks.md` includes Definition of Done and execution log.
