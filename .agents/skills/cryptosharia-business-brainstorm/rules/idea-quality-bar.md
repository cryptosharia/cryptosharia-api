# Idea Quality Bar

Use this quality bar for CryptoSharia business brainstorming output.

## Required in Every Option

- Clear user persona and problem statement.
- Trust and educational value, not speculation-first framing.
- Delivery feasibility through existing API-first architecture.
- Explicit risks and assumptions.

## Red Lines

- Do not imply guaranteed returns or certain financial outcomes.
- Do not overstate compliance certainty beyond current evidence.
- Do not propose shortcuts that weaken auth, moderation, or trust boundaries.

## Recommendation Standard

- Recommend low-risk experiment first.
- Define measurable success signals and a stop/go decision gate.
